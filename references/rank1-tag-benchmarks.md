# Rank-1 Tag Benchmarks & CTR Swipe File

Statistical guidelines and high-converting templates for Title Tags and Meta Descriptions.

---

## 1. Title Tag CTR Benchmarks

Google typically truncates titles exceeding **600 pixels** (roughly 55–60 characters).

### Top Performing Formulas:
1. **The Definitive Guide / Number Formula:**
   - `{Keyword} — The Complete Guide for 2026 | {Brand}`
   - `Top 10 {Keyword} Strategies That Actually Work | {Brand}`

2. **The Direct Solution / Action Formula:**
   - `Best {Keyword} for Modern Teams | Fast & Secure`
   - `How to Choose the Right {Keyword} (Step-by-Step)`

3. **Comparison / Versus Formula:**
   - `{Keyword} vs Alternatives: Full 2026 Comparison`

### High-Impact CTR Modifiers:
- `Guide`, `Review`, `Best`, `Top`, `Free`, `Checklist`, `Template`, `Pricing`, `2026`

---

## 2. Meta Description Guidelines

Google displays up to **960 pixels** on desktop (approx. 155–160 chars) and **680 pixels** on mobile (approx. 120 chars).

### Structure of a Winning Meta Description:
`[Problem / Pain Point] + [Concrete Value Proposition / What's Inside] + [Action-Oriented CTA]`

### Example Swipe File:
- *B2B / SaaS:*
  "Looking to streamline your {topic}? Discover how modern teams automate workflows, reduce errors, and scale faster. Try it free today." (138 chars)
- *Informational / Tutorial:*
  "Master {topic} in minutes with our comprehensive 2026 guide. Learn essential techniques, real-world examples, and expert tips. Read now." (138 chars)
- *Ecommerce / Product:*
  "Explore premium {product} crafted for durability and performance. Enjoy free shipping, guaranteed warranty, and easy 30-day returns. Shop now." (144 chars)
